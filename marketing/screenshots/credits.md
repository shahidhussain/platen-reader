# Books shown in the App Store screenshots

Put a short line in the App Store description, such as: "Sample books: public-domain classics, picture books from Book Dash (CC BY 4.0), and *Zoe and Zephy* by Shahid Hussain."

| Book | Where it shows | Rights |
|---|---|---|
| *Jane Eyre*, Charlotte Brontë | Home (favourite), read aloud | Public domain |
| *Dracula*, *Frankenstein*, *The Picture of Dorian Gray* and the other classics | Home (folder covers) | Public domain (authors died more than 70 years ago) |
| *The Tunnel Under the World*, Frederik Pohl; *Triplanetary*, E. E. Smith | Home (covers only) | Public domain in the US only: never shown open |
| *Lory Dory* and the other Book Dash picture books | Home (covers) | Book Dash, CC BY 4.0 (credit the authors and illustrators named in each book) |
| *Zoe and Zephy*, Shahid Hussain | The picture-book card | The author's own |

Left out of the screenshot library on purpose: *Brave New World* (in copyright in the US), *Harry Potter and the Methods of Rationality* (another company's trademark), and *Mother Goose*, *The Three Little Pigs* and the `public-rbc-…` scan (old stories in editions whose illustrations may be recent).
